# Library Borrowing Policy: General Rules

## Loan Limits
- Standard patrons may borrow up to **5 books** at any one time.
- Academic and research patrons may borrow up to **10 books**.

## Loan Period
- Standard book loan duration is **14 days** (2 weeks).
- High-demand or newly released titles carry a shortened loan period of **7 days**.
- Reference materials, rare manuscripts, and archival records are strictly **non-circulating** and must be used within the library reading room.

## Patron Responsibilities
- Patrons must present a valid library patron name or ID at the time of checkout.
- Borrowers are responsible for maintaining books in good physical condition.
- Any damaged or unreturned books after 30 days are classified as lost and billed to the borrower.
