# Library Borrowing Policy: Overdue Fines & Fees

## Overdue Rate
- Standard books: **$0.25 per day** per overdue item.
- Special reserve titles: **$1.00 per day** per overdue item.

## Grace Period
- A **2-day grace period** applies after the due date before daily fines begin accumulating.
- If items are returned within the grace period, all accrued fees are waived.

## Maximum Accrual & Borrowing Suspension
- Maximum fine per item is capped at **$15.00** or the replacement cost of the book, whichever is lower.
- Borrowing privileges are temporarily suspended if an account carries more than **$10.00** in unpaid fines.
- Replacement fees for lost books equal the catalog purchase price plus a $5.00 processing fee.
