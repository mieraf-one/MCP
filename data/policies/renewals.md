# Library Borrowing Policy: Loan Renewals

## Renewal Eligibility
- Eligible circulating titles may be renewed up to **2 consecutive times**.
- Each renewal extends the loan period by an additional **14 days** from the renewal date.

## Renewal Restrictions
- Books reserved or placed on hold by another patron **cannot be renewed**.
- Overdue items with accrued fines exceeding $5.00 cannot be renewed until fines are resolved.
- Items can be renewed online via the AI Library Assistant or at the front circulation desk.
